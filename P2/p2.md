# 1. The design problem, audience and value

Who Exactly: 

    Role: This project would benefit music-loving university students who want to get back into reading through a fun, personality-analyzation-style experience. It targets students comfortable with digital technology who want short, personalized poetry recpmmendations based on their recent music taste.

    Situation: The mindset of a user who opens this app would look like someone who listens to a lot of music and wants to find low-pressure, enjoyable ways to feel productive- such as reading; someone with recent motivation to start reading again, but doesn't want to immediately jump into long novels- rather they're looking for short reads to incorporate into their daily routine. 

    Trying to get done: They (users) want to see their current music taste analyzed with a few words, then receive a list of poems that match with those words.  

Why would they use this instead of what exists?:
There are a lot of existing apps that analyze users' listening history and attach their data to fun personality results, and there are a lot of different resources online to get recommended poetry picks, however there aren't any apps that I've found yet that do both in one go, causing extra work for any music fans trying to find poems that match with their current listening vibes. In contrast to what already exists, this app combines listening history analysis together with poetry recommendations, helping users easily find poems that feel personally relevant to their music taste.
