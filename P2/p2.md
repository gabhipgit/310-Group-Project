# 1. The design problem, audience and value (3 to 5 sentences)
Who exactly: their role, the mindset or situation they're in when they open your app, and what they're trying to get done.
Why would they use this instead of what exists?

Who Exactly: This project would benefit young-adult music lovers who are trying to get back into reading and want motivation. Specifically those who want a fun way to find short readings (in poem form) that they'd feel currently connected too. This would be targeted for people who like analyzing their music taste to see how it connects to their character, while also encouraging the return/increase of reading literature.
